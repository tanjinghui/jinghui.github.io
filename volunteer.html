I volunteer with the Kong Meng San Phor Kark See (KMSPKS) Monastery Bright Youth, where I have served since 2023 and became a Youth Leader (YL) in 2025. Below are the leadership projects I have co-led, from spotting a problem to seeing the solution adopted.

Youth Leader Onboarding Handbook Co-Project Lead

KMSPKS Bright Youth · June 2025 – April 2026

Problem

There was no onboarding process for incoming volunteer leaders. I diagnosed this in June 2025 by combining my own onboarding experience and conversations with peer YLs.

Action

  • June 2025Co-developed the first draft of the handbook with a fellow YL (co-Project Lead).
  • September 2025Presented the draft to the Staff Team Leader, framing it as a resource to strengthen the leadership team and the wider department.
  • September – November 2025Invited 4 YLs from 4 pillars to review the draft and give feedback.
  • September 2025 – January 2026Reworked the draft over 4 months with the peer YL and an intern, folding in feedback from all 4 peer reviewers.
  • January – February 2026Reviewed the draft with 3 Staff Team members to align it with operations, then refined it.
  • February 2026Reviewed with the wider Bright Youth Leadership Team before rollout to incoming YLs, and refined it further.
  • March 2026Reviewed the latest draft and next steps with the entire Staff Team, including the Team Lead.

Outcome

  • The handbook was integrated into the organisation's existing training framework (April 2026).
  • Piloted with 6 incoming YLs, with a focus on the “why” behind each practice rather than only the “what”.
  • Set up a decentralised Point-of-Contact system pairing incoming leaders with Staff Team members and Youth Leaders (3 of the peer reviewers), so new leaders know who to ask and feel safe asking.

Feedback received

  • A peer reviewer called the handbook “very comprehensive” and “at the same level as the industry standard handbooks”.
  • A Staff Team member asked, “Why did the previous generations of leaders not think of this?”

Intern Onboarding Handbook Co-Project Lead

KMSPKS Bright Youth · September 2025 – 2026

  • Appointed by the Staff Team Leader to co-lead a second handbook for interns (September 2025), built on the structure of the YL handbook.
  • Met the intern early on to agree how we would work together, as both project leads had just started university. Encouraged the intern to log daily tasks and projects, which became the basis for the documentation when we returned to it in December 2025.
  • Co-mentored the intern through both handbooks using a “guided-thinking” approach, which let them contribute directly to high-level documentation and organisational resources.

Outcome

  • The intern shared the working version of the handbook with the incoming intern (January 2026).
  • In March 2026 the Staff Team Leader asked us to guide the next intern batch (September 2026) using the handbook.
